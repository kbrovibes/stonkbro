# v0.44.1 — Open contracts showed the wrong strike after a roll

## What broke
The Portfolio page's Open tab was reported showing "old contracts." An option position that gets rolled (buy-to-close the current contract, sell-to-open a new strike/expiry) is tracked internally as one continuous chain across all its rolls. The row's ticker label (`contractLabel`) read strike and quantity off `firstContractLeg` — the very first SELL/BUY leg the chain ever had — while the row's caption separately computed the *current* expiry via `findOpenLeg`. For a chain that's rolled several times, that split the label from reality: a short NBIS call opened 2026-06-25 at a $350 strike and rolled about ten times since showed as "NBIS 350C × 5" (a contract closed months ago) with the correct current expiry printed right next to it.

## Investigation
Cross-checked against SnapTrade's live API directly (`listOptionHoldings`, ground truth for currently-open contracts) rather than assuming a sync problem:
- SnapTrade's Fidelity connection was healthy, transactions synced through 2026-09-24.
- The 7 live open option holdings matched the 7 chains our derivation marked `OPEN`, unit-for-unit.
- So the cached chain scan and the roll-pairing/lineage logic in `getOptionChains` were both already correct — the bug was purely in how the Open row's label was rendered from an already-correct chain.

## Fix
- `src/app/(app)/portfolio/page.tsx`: added `openContractLabel(chain, openLeg)`, which builds the ticker label from the chain's currently-live leg (already computed by `findOpenLeg` for the caption) instead of the chain's original opening leg. `openRowModel` now uses it. Closed/assigned/LEAPS rows keep using `contractLabel` unchanged — LEAPS chains never roll (only short chains participate in roll pairing), and for closed/assigned rows labeling by the trade's original contract is the intended history view.
