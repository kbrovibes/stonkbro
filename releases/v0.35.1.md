# v0.35.1 — Briefing player polish + retention

## Changes

- **Explicit speed options**: replaced the cycling speed button with a segmented control — 1x · 1.25x · 1.5x pills, active speed highlighted. Applies immediately to the playing audio.
- **7-day retention sweep**: new `deleteExpiredBriefings()` in `src/lib/db/briefings.ts` deletes briefing rows *and* their MP3s from the `briefings` bucket once `briefing_date` falls outside the 7-day history window. Runs best-effort after each successful cron generation (`/api/cron/briefing`). Previously the player only listed the last 7 days but storage grew unbounded.

## Notes

- The history playlist itself (date + title + duration + saved indicator per prior day) shipped in v0.35.0 and is unchanged.
- Files: `src/components/briefing/BriefingPlayer.tsx`, `src/lib/db/briefings.ts`, `src/app/api/cron/briefing/route.ts`.
