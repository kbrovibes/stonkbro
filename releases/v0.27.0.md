# v0.27.0 — Privacy lock: hand your phone to a friend

## Problem

Showing someone the app means showing them everything: portfolio value,
P&L, collateral, position sizes, account balances. There was no way to let
a friend play with the scanner or Learn without disclosing wealth.

## Design

- **Lock**: one tap in the profile-menu dropdown. **Unlock**: in-app PIN
  modal. The PIN (4-8 digits) is per-user, set in Settings → Privacy Lock,
  stored in `user_settings.privacy_pin` (RLS; never returned by the API —
  GET exposes only `has_privacy_pin`).
- **Server-verified**: unlock POSTs the code to `/api/privacy`; comparison
  happens server-side, wrong codes get a 600ms damper, and the lock state
  is an **httpOnly cookie** — page JS can't clear it, and server-rendered
  HTML is masked at render time, not painted over.
- **PIN is immutable while locked** — otherwise the person holding the
  locked phone could overwrite it via the settings API.
- Threat model: casual (hand the phone over), not adversarial/devtools.

## What masks (→ `•••••`)

- **Portfolio**: year P&L hero, collateral locked, per-chain/leg amounts,
  breakevens, real strikes + contract counts, live quotes on held
  contracts, monthly P&L, LEAPS entries, chart y-axes + tooltips
- **Hindsight/Time Machine**: the "you'd have $X more" hero, per-account
  balances AND account last-4, holdings tables + footers, share counts,
  deposits/withdrawals/RSU vests, realized gains, tax estimates, option
  replay rows; the detail page's dollars toggle forces hidden while locked
- **Income**: starting capital (the single most direct wealth number),
  premium totals, monthly bars · **Dashboard**: P&L + income cards ·
  **Positions**: income card, strikes, entry prices, quantities
- **Wheel**: incomes and cycle histories ("Assigned at $X — 100 shares" →
  redacted) · **Covered Calls**: real share counts + cost basis
- **Alert banner**: market-monitor alerts can say "you hold this" with
  dollar levels — body hides behind "🔒 Alert hidden" while locked

## Deliberately visible

Percentages and ratios (no absolute anchor), market quotes, the
hypothetical $100K scanner/portfolio-manager math, Learn, research,
watchlists, the ticker roster of holdings pages, and forms mid-entry.
Push/email content is out of scope: the lock protects the device in hand,
not the inbox.

## Verification

- Adversarial leak review over the full diff (remaining `$`-renders
  audited file-by-file), `tsc` clean, production build passes.
- Note: ticker names of held positions remain visible by design — masking
  the roster would require curtaining /wheel and /covered-calls entirely.
