# v0.26.0 — Learn v2: real-data case studies + offline hardening

## Problem

Learn's coursework taught concepts on synthetic data. The scanner had
meanwhile produced three months of real history (May–Aug 2026) full of
teachable outcomes — puts sold at exact tops, "support floors" that failed
in a day, -50% drawdowns, wheels that recovered and wheels that didn't —
none of it used. Separately, offline behavior was inconsistent: Learn was
cached but network pages hung on spinners, and Portfolio showed nothing
without a connection.

## What shipped

### Case Studies track (spec 56)

15 ticker modules / 80 case studies mined entirely from the committed
chain-snapshot dataset (`src/lib/learn/v2-data/`): 10 traded-universe
tickers (PLTR, SOFI, NBIS, MRVL, TSLA, TSM, ASTS, SNDK, META, RKLB) and
5 "opportunities you weren't watching" (NVDA, AMD, CRWD, HOOD, IWM).

Data integrity is enforced structurally: content files reference the
dataset only through typed helpers (`src/lib/learn/v2.ts` —
`findPut`/`findCall`/`barOn`/`contractSeries`), and a lookup that isn't in
the dataset **throws at build time**. A fabricated strike, premium, date,
or Greek fails `next build` instead of shipping. No personal
position/account data appears anywhere.

Each study follows Setup (dated real snapshot) → Decision point → What
actually happened (real price path / later snapshots) → Lesson, across
archetypes:

- **Stop-loss discipline** on real collapses (ASTS -60%, RKLB -61%,
  SNDK -56%, MRVL -48%, PLTR -33%)
- **Stop-market vs stop-limit** through genuinely wide spreads (TSM's
  34%-wide quote, AMD/CRWD illiquid rows)
- **Poor contract selection** — AROC chasing, delta too high, earnings-eve
  entries, 1-lot liquidity
- **Verified winners** — high-juiciness entries whose price paths confirmed
  the thesis
- **Roll vs assignment** — including TSLA's grind-down where assignment
  never recovered (the anti-survivorship counterexample to PLTR's wheel)
- **Covered-call strike regret** on the rippers (META, IWM)
- **Observed theta/IV drift** — real multi-day premium series on IWM/AMD

### Four new interactive components

- **CasePriceChart** — real OHLC bars + strike/support lines + dated event
  markers
- **PlaceTheStop** — drag a stop onto the real chart with the future
  hidden, then reveal what happened
- **ContractPicker** — A/B choice between two real scanned contracts with
  full quote/Greeks rows
- **PremiumDecayScrubber** — scrub a contract's actual scanned premium/IV
  across days

41 interactive sections and 62 quizzes across the track. Landing page gets
a Case Studies ticker grid (traded vs opportunity badges, per-module
progress); completion uses the existing `learn_progress` mechanism, and
the offline manifest picks the ~95 new routes up automatically (SW v4).

### Offline mode hardening (spec 57)

- **Shared state** (`src/lib/offline.ts`): `useOffline()` OR's
  `navigator.onLine` with a manual flag flipped by `reportNetworkError()`,
  so flaky connections that still claim onLine register as offline.
- **Fail-fast**: `OfflineGate` wraps 10 network-dependent pages (scanner,
  CSP Hunter, research, portfolio-manager, bloodbath, time-machine,
  options, signals, earnings, explosive) — instant "needs a connection"
  state with retry instead of a hung spinner.
- **Portfolio offline snapshot**: last successful payload cached
  browser-locally (`src/lib/client-cache.ts`), rendered read-only with an
  "as of" timestamp when offline. Nothing committed to the repo.
- **SW v4**: version bump + stale-cache cleanup; Learn v1+v2 fully
  pre-cached via the manifest.
