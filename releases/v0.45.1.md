# v0.45.1 — Open Contracts cards show strike/DTE without expanding

## What changed
The Portfolio page's Open tab card (`ChainCard` in `src/app/(app)/portfolio/page.tsx`, classic theme — the default) only showed strike and expiry inside the tap-to-expand leg table (`Open: $280 2026-12-18`, buried under the "Get Live Quote" section). The collapsed head showed underlying, option type, a `SHORT`/`LONG` badge, an `OPEN`/`CLOSED`/etc. status badge, roll count, and institution — but nothing about the actual contract.

## Fix
- `ChainCard` now takes an `isOpenTab` prop. On the Open tab:
  - The head shows the currently-open strike and DTE inline (`findOpenLeg` was already computing the live strike for the expanded section — reused here, plus a `daysBetween(today, expiry)` DTE calc).
  - The `SHORT`/`LONG` and status (`OPEN`) badges are hidden — both are redundant on a tab where every row is already open, by definition, and SELL/BUY-first-ness isn't worth a badge on its own.
  - Closed/Assigned tabs are untouched — status varies there, so the badge still carries information.
- `openRowModel` (refresh theme's row builder) caption now shows `${dte} DTE` instead of the raw `exp <date>` string, matching the classic theme and pairing with the ticker's live strike from v0.44.1.
