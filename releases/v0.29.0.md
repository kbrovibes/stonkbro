# v0.29.0 — Potential Year Total, Per-Brokerage Chains, Tax Center

## Potential Year Total (Portfolio summary)

New row under "Year so far": `realized YTD + Σ net_pnl of open SELL-direction chains`, i.e. what the year lands at if every open CSP/CC expires worthless in the user's favor.

- Only shorts (`open_units < 0`) count — a long chain expiring worthless is max loss, not upside.
- Only open legs expiring on/after today AND within the current calendar year — premium on a Jan-2027 expiry realizes next year.
- Shows the unsettled component (`+$X unsettled`) and count; masked under the privacy lock like all wealth values.

## Per-brokerage chains (`src/lib/snaptrade/client.ts`)

- `OptionChain.institution?: string` — populated from the account each activity came from (`_institution` tag applied per-account before flattening).
- Contract grouping key and roll-pairing display groups now include institution, so identical contracts at two brokerages stay separate chains and rolls never pair across brokers.
- Field is optional: cached chain scans written before this release lack it; UI degrades gracefully (no badge) until the next refresh/cron scan.

## Open tab: brokerage badge + filter

- Violet institution badge on each chain card (all tabs); on the Open tab it's clickable — tap to filter to that brokerage, tap again to clear. A "Broker" pill row above the list mirrors the filter. Rendered only when chains carry `institution`.

## Tax Center

New `/taxes` page (ProfileMenu → Tax Center) + `GET/POST/DELETE /api/taxes` + `tax_payments` table (migration `20260815210000_tax_payments.sql`, applied to prod 2026-08-15, RLS on `auth.uid()`).

**Model** (`src/lib/taxes/config.ts`, `estimates.ts` — pure functions):
- Assumes ~$700K W-2 income fully withheld → trading tax is incremental at top marginal rates: STCG 40.8% (37% + 3.8% NIIT), LTCG 23.8% federal, WA 7% excise only on LTCG above the ~$280K exemption (STCG never WA-taxed).
- Term classification: SELL-direction chains always short-term (IRC §1234(b)); BUY-direction long-term only if start→end > 365 days.
- Realized events = chains with status CLOSED/EXPIRED/ASSIGNED and `end_date` in tax-year 2026, bucketed into IRS estimated-tax periods (Apr 15 / Jun 15 / Sep 15 / Jan 15 2027 due dates).
- `recommended(N) = max(0, taxOn(cumulative STCG/LTCG through N) − payments due on/before N)`, with cross-bucket loss netting floored at $0 — later losses and earlier overpayments automatically reduce later recommendations. Payments bucket to the first period whose due date ≥ paid date.

**UI**: year summary cards (net realized, est. tax, paid, remaining), per-period cards with the current period's "Pay $X by <date>" highlighted, past-period over/underpaid deltas, STCG/LTCG split, top-5 underlyings, record-a-payment form + deletable payment list, collapsible assumptions panel with safe-harbor explainer and not-tax-advice disclaimer. Privacy lock masks all dollar values.

**Known limits**: assignment stock-side P&L untracked (options-only net_pnl); wash sales and $3K ordinary-loss offset unmodeled (net-loss years floor at $0 tax); WA exemption approximate.
