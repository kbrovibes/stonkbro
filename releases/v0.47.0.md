# v0.47.0 — Paper Lab bots get a full-history Portfolio tab

## What broke
Reported: opening `/paper/margin-bull` showed nothing under Trades. Root cause — the Soul/Positions/Trades/Journal tabs on a bot's page are all scoped to a single selected day (via the date-chip picker at the top), sourced from `GET /api/paper/[profileId]?date=YYYY-MM-DD`. If the bot made no trades on whichever day happened to be selected (defaults to the most recent date with a snapshot), the Trades tab was empty even though the bot has a real, ongoing trading history — confirmed margin-bull has 24 trades across Sep 9–24 and 4 closed positions with a real -$5,570 realized loss, none of which was visible without manually clicking through every day chip.

## What was actually requested
Treat each of the 10 paper bots as a full-fledged trader with a real portfolio: every trade ever made, lot-level entry prices for accumulated (DCA) positions, and realized gains/losses per closed position — not just a day-by-day snapshot.

## Why no new cost-basis engine was needed
`paper_positions` already stores `avg_price` (blended cost, updated on every add-to-position) and books `realized_pnl` + `close_price` at the moment a position closes (`src/lib/paper/broker.ts`). `paper_trades` already stores every individual trade at its own price/qty/date. So "what price did each DCA buy happen at" is just the raw trade log (no FIFO lot math needed), and "realized gain per closed trade" is already computed and stored per position — it just had no query or UI surfacing it beyond the single-day `account.realizedPnl` aggregate.

## What shipped
- `src/lib/db/paper.ts`: `getAllTradesFor(profileId)` and `getAllPositionsFor(profileId)` — both/either without a date filter, ordered chronologically.
- `src/app/api/paper/[profileId]/portfolio/route.ts` (new): returns the full trade history and full position history (open + closed) for a profile. Separate from the main day-scoped route so it's only fetched when the new tab is opened, not on every page load.
- `src/app/(app)/paper/[profileId]/PortfolioTab.tsx` (new): summary stats (realized P&L, closed count, win rate, total trades), a new sortable `ClosedPositionsTable` (contract, type, qty, avg entry, exit, opened, closed, realized $, return %), and the full trade history via the existing sortable `TradesList`.
- `src/app/(app)/paper/[profileId]/ProfileScreen.tsx` / `ProfileSections.tsx`: wired in a new "Portfolio" tab; `TabDef.count` made optional since this tab has no natural single count to badge.
