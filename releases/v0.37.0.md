# v0.37.0 — Tri-daily briefings + hourly crash alerts

## Problem

A market-wide selloff went unannounced: the market-monitor cron (which pushes SPY-drop alerts at −1.25%/−2%) had been throttled to once daily at 10am PT since the Hobby-plan days, and the single pre-market briefing generated at 6am — before the damage was visible in quotes.

## Changes

- **Sessions**: `daily_briefings.session` (`premarket` | `midday` | `close`; migration `20260818150000`). Crons: `/api/cron/briefing?session=premarket` at 13:00 UTC, `?session=midday` at 14:30 UTC (one hour after the bell), `?session=close` at 21:00 UTC (after-hours trends). Skip logic is per date+session (`hasCompletedSession`); manual regenerates infer the session from Eastern time (`sessionForNow`).
- **Prompts**: each session carries its own guidance (`BRIEFING_SESSIONS[].focus`) — pre-open setup vs. how-the-open-went vs. day recap + after-hours. System prompt gains a non-negotiable lead-with-the-shock rule for ±1.5% SPY/QQQ moves.
- **Player**: header and playlist rows show the session label alongside the hour stamp.
- **Monitor**: `market-monitor` cron `0 17 * * 1-5` → `35 13-20 * * 1-5` (hourly, market hours).

## Notes

- All three episodes stack in the playlist for the day; 7-day retention unchanged (≈15 episodes / ~15 MB of audio per week).
