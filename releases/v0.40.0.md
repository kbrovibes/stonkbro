# v0.40.0 — LEAPS Lab + PMCC Income · Paper Trading · Home card grid

## Summary
Three features in one release. The refresh home returns to a dense ticker card grid with sparklines and gets the briefing (podcast) entry point back. The Options page grows a LEAPS Lab (daily scan + quarter × stock-move return grid with pins, custom strikes and two-strike comparison) and a PMCC Income finder (rank setups by monthly income for a capital budget with an exact LEAPS-vs-short-call breakdown). Paper Trading adds ten rule-driven bots trading three times a day on live data with a leaderboard, per-bot portfolios and end-of-day notes.

## Notes
- Both migrations were applied to the stonkbro Supabase project; the first LEAPS scan (25 LEAPS, 30 PMCC, 110 tickers, 0 errors) and the first paper session (close, 2026-09-08, all ten bots traded) were run so the pages open with data.
- The LEAPS grid holds IV constant at each contract's latest value and uses r = 4%; unlisted or unpriced strikes are reported as unavailable, never estimated.
- Paper bots are idempotent per (date, session): a re-run re-marks and re-snapshots without trading. Market holidays are not detected.
- The Growth Shadow basket is a proxy for an iofund-style concentrated growth book, not the fund's actual holdings.
