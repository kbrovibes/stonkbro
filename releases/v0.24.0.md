# v0.24.0 — Bloodbath (pullback navigator)

## New

### `/bloodbath` page
Mobile-first pullback dashboard:
- Summary strip: tickers scanned, count down 10%+, worst hit
- **Your Watchlist**: every watchlist ticker with drawdown off its 4-week peak close, today's move, 4-week change, 50-SMA flag, volume ratio — sorted worst first
- **Big Market Drops**: non-watchlist names from the ~150-symbol scan universe down ≥10% from their 4-week peak (max 15)
- Tap-to-expand cards: AI reasons for the drop, a dip verdict chip (Buy the dip / Nibble / Wait / Avoid) with confidence, a concrete entry idea, peak price/date, link to full ticker view

### API
- `GET /api/bloodbath` — scan: watchlist ∪ scan universe → quotes → Tradier 30-day history for candidates → drawdown math. History fetches capped at 60/scan (watchlist always included; market names pre-filtered by below-50-SMA or −3% day).
- `POST /api/bloodbath/verdict` (auth) — up to 12 tickers, 3 Yahoo headlines each, ONE batched AI call (`generateText`, 8k tokens, feature=`bloodbath`) returning strict-JSON verdicts with truncation repair.

## Changed

- **Home page**: IPO widget removed. `UpcomingCatalysts` is earnings-only now. A 3-up feature-card grid (🩸 Bloodbath, 📊 Portfolio, ⏰ Hindsight) sits at the top of the page.
- **MoreNav**: Bloodbath added to the Discover group.
