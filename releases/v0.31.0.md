# v0.31.0 — Tax Center: Stock Sales + Per-Quarter ST/LT

## Why

The Tax Center only computed from option chains; equity sells (which also trigger gains/losses, short or long) were invisible, and the ST/LT split only rendered when LT was nonzero.

## Data reality (verified against live SnapTrade feeds)

- BUY / SELL / REI activities carry date, units, price, amount at both brokers → FIFO basis + holding period computable for anything bought at the broker.
- TRANSFER / JOURNALED share inflows arrive at price 0 with **no basis and no original acquisition date** (RSU vests, broker-to-broker moves — e.g. the NVDA/AMZN transfers into Chase in May 2026). Sales matched to those lots cannot be taxed correctly from feed data alone.
- LOAN rows are fully-paid securities lending (non-taxable, nets out) — ignored. Sweep funds (SPAXX, QACDS, …) churn at $1.00 — excluded.

## Engine (`src/lib/taxes/equities.ts`, pure)

`computeEquitySales(taggedActivities)`: per (broker|symbol) FIFO queues; BUY/REI push lots (amount-derived per-share cost incl. fees); TRANSFER-in pushes an unknown-basis lot; TRANSFER-out removes FIFO without realizing; SELL matches lots → per-lot gain and term (>365 days = long), unknown portions accumulate as `unknownUnits/unknownProceeds`. Deterministic event keys `broker|symbol|date|units[#n]` for overrides.

`equityRealizedEvents(sales, overrides, year)`: converts matched portions to `RealizedEvent{source:"equity"}` fed into the existing period/year math (which is source-agnostic); unknown portions become `needsBasis` items unless a user override (total basis + acquired date) resolves them.

Sanity-verified: 120-share sell across a 2024 lot + 2026 lot splits 100 LT / 20 ST with exact basis; transfer-in sale lands in needsBasis; override converts it to LTCG.

## Storage / API

- `tax_equity_scans` (cached FIFO scan results, service-role written) + `tax_basis_overrides` (per-user RLS, unique per event key) — migration `20260816120000_tax_equities.sql`, applied to prod.
- `POST /api/taxes/equities` — sync now: `getAllActivitiesTagged("2010-01-01")` under a tracked, cancellable job (`tax-equity-sync`); `GET` returns scan freshness.
- `POST/DELETE /api/taxes/basis` — basis overrides.
- `/api/taxes` GET merges equity events into `computePeriods`/`computeYearSummary`; response adds `equities: { lastSyncedAt, saleCount, needsBasis }` and periods gain `stcgOptions/stcgEquity/ltcgOptions/ltcgEquity`.

## UI

- Period cards: ST and LT rows always shown, each with `options $A · stocks $B` sub-split; amber per-quarter banner when sales are missing basis.
- Stock Sales panel: sync button (job-tracked, minutes), last-synced stamp, needs-basis list with inline total-basis + acquired-date form; year ST/LT card now always visible.

## Limitations

- Wash sales, stock splits (shortfall lands in needs-basis rather than corrupting math), spinoff basis allocation, and dividends are not modeled.
- Equity events only exist after the first sync; scan staleness is shown, not auto-refreshed.
