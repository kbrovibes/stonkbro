# v0.24.5 — Fix Portfolio page timeout after v0.24.4 rate-limit pacing

## Problem

The Portfolio page timed out on load. Its main data call —
`GET /api/portfolio?include=option-chains&startDate=2025-01-01` — ran under
`maxDuration = 30`, but v0.24.4 deliberately slowed the underlying activities
fetch to survive SnapTrade's per-minute cap: split-level calls are paced 2.6s
apart and a terminal 429 waits out a full 65s rate-limit window. With 1.5+
years of history the option-chain pull recurses past the 1000-record cap into
several split levels, so the request now legitimately runs well over 30s —
Vercel killed the function and the page saw a timeout.

v0.24.4 left `getOptionChains` untouched on the assumption it "doesn't
recurse deep" over a 2026+ window, but the Portfolio page fetches from
2025-01-01.

## Fix

- `GET /api/portfolio`: `maxDuration` 30 → 300 (Vercel allows 300s on all
  plans). The paced fetch completes instead of being killed mid-flight.
- `GET /api/portfolio/time-machine`: `maxDuration` 60 → 300 — a single 429
  wait alone is 65s, so 60s guaranteed a timeout whenever SnapTrade
  throttled the full-history pull.
- `getOptionChains`: accounts are now walked sequentially, same as
  `getAllActivities` in v0.24.4. The old `Promise.all` fan-out ran one paced
  recursion tree per account concurrently, and their combined request rate
  could still burst the per-minute cap — triggering the 65s wait and making
  the timeout worse.

## Trade-off

Worst-case Portfolio load is slower (sequential accounts), but it completes
reliably. The right longer-term improvement is caching the computed chains
(as bloodbath does with `bloodbath_scans`) so page loads don't hit SnapTrade
live at all.
