# v0.49.0 — Earnings Play

## What was requested
A feature that watches each week's earnings reports and suggests option strategies using trends, news, signals and charts. For MU specifically: four strategies, the max gain and loss on a 5–10% post-earnings move assuming a close a day or so after, and a clear explanation of each contract and why it's recommended.

## Design
- **Horizon is the exit, not expiry.** Every number is P&L if closed at midday the first session after the report. Expiry is the first listed one after that exit, so legs still carry time value.
- **IV crush is modeled, not assumed.** Front-expiry ATM IV σ₁ and next-expiry IV σ₂ give base vol σ_b² = (σ₂²T₂ − σ₁²T₁)/(T₂ − T₁). Every leg's IV is scaled by σ_b/σ₁ at exit. Falls back to 30-day realized vol that excludes past earnings days.
- **Weekend-correct clock.** Chains fetched outside market hours carry the last session's IVs, so time-to-expiry is measured from that close.
- **Ranking answers the user's question.** Average P&L over +5…+10% and −10…−5% (70/30 toward the lean, 50/50 when neutral) ÷ capital at risk.
- **Liquid strikes.** Strikes with ≥50 open interest are preferred whenever both sides of the chain have enough of them. (MU's first pick, the $1082.5, had OI 6; it's now the $1080 with OI 1,154.)
- **Real report history.** Nasdaq's earnings-surprise endpoint supplies true report dates and EPS surprises. Reactions are measured on the session that actually reacted (next day for after-close reports). The earlier "biggest move ±12 days around every 91 days" proxy was wrong for MU (it picked non-earnings days), so it's now only a labeled fallback.
