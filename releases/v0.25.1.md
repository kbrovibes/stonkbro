# v0.25.1 — Learn overhaul: real interactivity + practice-first TA coursework

## Problem

The Learn feature read as "overly simple or not interactive/interesting":
the schema's "interactive" sections were aliased to static charts (a
strike-slider rendered a fixed delta curve), charts drew toy data with
almost no controls, and lessons were theory-first with few worked examples
and no practice. After the v0.25.0 perf change, unvisited lessons were also
unreachable offline (content moved server-side but the offline manifest
only pre-cached module pages).

## What shipped

**Real interactive components** (self-contained SVG, seeded synthetic data,
zero network — fully offline-capable):
- **LevelFinder** — tap/drag to place a support/resistance line on a
  candlestick chart; scored against the answer zone with retry, hints, and
  reveal; difficulty 1–3 and a "New chart" reseed for drilling.
- **IndicatorPlayground** — one price series with toggleable SMA20/50/200,
  EMA21, Bollinger(20,2), RSI(14) and MACD panes, period sliders, and
  auto-annotated golden/death crosses, RSI 30/70 crossings, and squeeze
  regions. Presets: trend / meanreversion / squeeze / divergence.
- **StrikeExplorer** — strike/DTE/IV sliders drive live Black-Scholes
  premium, delta, theta/day, breakeven, approximate POP, and a payoff
  diagram with a plain-English readout.
- **ChartQuiz** — spot-the-setup multiple choice on rendered scenario
  charts (support-bounce, breakout-retest, golden/death-cross,
  rsi-divergence, bb-squeeze) with the tell annotated after answering.

The old fake aliases (strike-slider, position-builder, greek-calculator…)
now open the real sandbox.

**TA track rewritten (31 → 37 lessons)** around a fixed arc per concept:
what it is (the market mechanic) → how to see it (exact plotting
click-paths, daily vs weekly) → 3+ worked examples including one labeled
failure with the early tell → 2+ interactive practice reps → how to trade
it (CSP strikes under support, covered calls at resistance, invalidation)
→ honest failure modes → quiz. Support & resistance got the deepest
treatment (6 lessons incl. graded drawing practice). 41 interactive
sections placed across the track.

**Greeks + practical tracks enriched**: every Greeks module embeds a
guided StrikeExplorer task (build the CSP by delta, the PMCC two-leg, the
0.75-delta LEAPS) plus 11 new worked examples; fluency lessons get
chart-quiz practice matched to each decoded phrase; leverage lessons
reproduce their own math in the sandbox.

**Offline fixed for lessons**: /api/learn/manifest now lists all ~115
lesson paths; SW v3 warms them with a 6h/manifest-version throttle.

**Structure**: curriculum.ts is now a thin assembler; content lives in
per-track files (content/greeks.ts, ta.ts, fluency.ts, leverage.ts) —
22 modules, ~120 lessons total. All pre-existing module/lesson ids kept
stable so progress records survive.
