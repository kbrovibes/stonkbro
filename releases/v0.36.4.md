# v0.36.4 — Briefing player control symmetry

## Change

The controls row in `src/components/briefing/BriefingPlayer.tsx` was a single centered flex row, so the wide speed-pill group on the left pushed the play/pause button off-center relative to the cover art. Rebuilt as three zones — `flex-1` left flank (speed pills), fixed center group (↺15 / play / 15↻), `flex-1` right flank (download, right-aligned) — equal flanks keep play/pause dead-center at every viewport width.
